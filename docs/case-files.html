01

The Wrong Account

Two members share almost-identical IDs — 8830142 and 8830124. A routine search can return both.

The robot clicks whichever row loads first. Money reaches a stranger's account before anyone notices.

WITH TANDEM

A container-scoped guard re-reads the exact member ID inside the confirmation box — not the search list — one step before the click.

ENTITY_BINDING_MISMATCH
HUMAN'S ROLEActive

A reviewer confirms the correct identity before the case is retried.

02

Did It Actually Go Through?

The submit screen freezes mid-request. Client and server disagree on what just happened.

Blind retry risks paying twice. Blind abandonment risks paying never — and nobody notices either way.

WITH TANDEM

An independent postcheck queries the target system directly. If even that can't confirm it, the case is frozen instead of guessed at.

UNCERTAIN_EFFECT
HUMAN'S ROLEActive

A person checks the bank's real records and tells the system what actually happened.

03

The Process Just Died

A hard crash lands right after money moves but before the legally-required notice is sent.

Restarting from zero either re-pays the credit or forgets the notice entirely — both are real violations.

WITH TANDEM

Every step is written to a durable, append-only ledger the instant it happens. A fresh process resumes from exactly where it stopped.

COMPLETED
HUMAN'S ROLENot needed

None. The ledger is the source of truth; a person can audit it after the fact.

04

A Human Has To Look

A compliance hold (fraud / BSA review) legally requires a person's sign-off before continuing.

Automation and a person can end up acting on the same live session at once, with no coordination.

WITH TANDEM

A single-owner lease with a fencing token means only one actor — robot or human — can hold a case at a time.

NEEDS_HUMAN
HUMAN'S ROLEActive

Entirely the human's step: review the case, sign off, hand control back.

05

Two Workers, One Case

Running automation across multiple machines can let two workers grab the same dispute at once.

Both post the credit. The customer is paid twice, and the bank eats the loss.

WITH TANDEM

An atomic database claim lets exactly one worker own a case at a time — a real lock, not a convention.

LEASE_FENCED
HUMAN'S ROLENot needed

None in the normal case. A stale claim is reassigned automatically and logged for later audit.

06

Someone Edited The Instructions

The robot's exact steps live in a saved file. What if someone quietly changes the destination account?

The tampered file runs identically to the real one. Nothing on screen looks any different.

WITH TANDEM

Every instruction file carries a cryptographic fingerprint. One changed character breaks it — the file is refused outright.

ARTIFACT_HASH_MISMATCH
HUMAN'S ROLEActive

Engineering is alerted to investigate why the fingerprint changed — it never silently continues.

07

The Bait-and-Switch Field

A page can display "$340" while a hidden field underneath actually holds a different number entirely.

Money moves for whatever the hidden field says — not what anyone approved on screen.

WITH TANDEM

The guard reads the literal value about to be submitted, not the rendered text, immediately before the click.

AMOUNT_MISMATCH
HUMAN'S ROLENot needed

None. The mismatch is blocked automatically and logged for review.

08

Garbage In, Money Out

Ordinary floating-point math can let invalid values — even "not-a-number" — slip past a dollar-limit check.

An invalid amount bypasses the $500 safety cap entirely.

WITH TANDEM

All money math runs on exact decimal arithmetic that rejects anything that isn't a clean, finite dollar amount.

POLICY_VIOLATION
HUMAN'S ROLENot needed

None. Malformed amounts never reach a real system.

09

Missing The Legal Clock

Regulation E sets hard day-counts — 2, 10, 45 days. Skipping only weekends ignores bank holidays and timezones.

A notice goes out a day late. That's a compliance violation, not a bug report.

WITH TANDEM

A real federal holiday calendar, per-institution timezone, and standard vs. extended case rules compute the true due date.

OVERDUE monitor
HUMAN'S ROLEActive

Compliance staff watch the deadline dashboard and act before a case goes overdue.

10

Anyone Could Reach In

Early on, admin controls — reset data, flip a switch, seize a case — had no lock on them at all.

Anyone reachable over the network could wipe live financial data with one request.

WITH TANDEM

Every sensitive control now requires a secret admin token. No token, flat refusal — every time.

401 UNAUTHORIZED
HUMAN'S ROLENot needed

Day-to-day operators never touch the token; only administrators hold it, by design.
